Rally Reel – Privacy

Rally Reel records volleyball games and makes highlight videos.

Your videos

Videos are uploaded straight from your phone to your own Google Drive, into a folder called "Volleyball Highlights". Rally Reel only has access to files it created (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

So that friends in the same room can combine angles, each game folder is shared as "anyone with the link can view". The links are only stored inside your rooms. You can delete any game at any time from the app or from Google Drive.

Rooms

When you use a room, your chosen display name, the room name and code, and the Drive file IDs of footage you add to that room are stored in a Google Firebase database so the other people in the room can see them. Rally Reel has no accounts, ads or tracking.
